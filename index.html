Welcome to Lumière Stories

Not Just Photographs. Memories That Last a Lifetime.

Every wedding has a story worth remembering. At LUMIÈRE STORIES, we capture the emotions, details and beautiful little moments that make every celebration unique.

We are a small, dedicated team of photographers and filmmakers based in Sangli. We work quietly in the background — noticing the glance between two families, the nervous laugh before the pheras, the light falling on a grandmother's hands — and we shape those moments into a story you will want to return to for years.

Featured Wedding Story

A Day Filled With Forever.

Ananya & Rohan · Sangli

The Couple
Ananya & Rohan
Location
Sangli, Maharashtra
Celebration
Three days · Haldi, Wedding, Reception
Style
Candid & Cinematic

Ananya and Rohan met at a friend's wedding in Kolhapur — so it felt right that their own celebration was all about family, laughter and the people who brought them together.

We followed three days of rituals in their hometown of Sangli: turmeric-stained hands at the haldi, a sea of marigolds at the mandap and a reception that ended with both grandfathers on the dance floor. We photographed it the way it felt — unhurried, warm and full of small, honest moments.

About the Studio

Behind Every Beautiful Story Is a Passion for Photography.

LUMIÈRE STORIES began with a simple belief: the best wedding photographs are the ones that make you feel something years later.

Our Philosophy

We document weddings honestly. We guide when it helps, step back when it matters, and never let the camera get in the way of the celebration.
